SUPPLY FAN (FICTIONAL TRAINING MANUAL)

The supply fan pushes conditioned air into the ductwork. Low supply airflow
with NORMAL filter pressure often points to the fan rather than the filter.

Common causes: a worn or slipping drive belt, a fan running slower than
commanded, or a supply damper stuck partly closed.

Belt inspection interval: every 6 months.

Inspection steps for an engineer:
1. Isolate the unit using the site lockout procedure.
2. Check belt tension, wear and alignment.
3. Compare the commanded fan speed with the measured fan speed.
4. Listen for bearing noise and check that the supply damper opens fully.
