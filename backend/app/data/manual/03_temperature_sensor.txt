ROOM TEMPERATURE SENSOR (FICTIONAL TRAINING MANUAL)

A faulty or badly placed sensor can report a temperature that does not match
the room. The control system then cools too much or too little.

Warning signs: occupant complaints that do not match the reported value,
sudden jumps in the reading, or a sensor mounted near a window, a heat source,
or in direct sunlight.

Calibration interval: every 12 months.

Inspection steps for an engineer:
1. Measure the room with a calibrated handheld thermometer.
2. Compare the handheld value with the sensor reading.
3. Check the sensor location and wiring before replacing it.
