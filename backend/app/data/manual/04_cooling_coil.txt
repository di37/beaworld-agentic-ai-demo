COOLING COIL (FICTIONAL TRAINING MANUAL)

The cooling coil removes heat from the air. A dirty coil, or too little
chilled water flowing through it, reduces cooling capacity.

Typical symptoms: supply air warmer than design with NORMAL airflow, and only
a small temperature difference between chilled water entering and leaving the coil.

Cleaning interval: every 12 months.

Inspection steps for an engineer:
1. Isolate the unit using the site lockout procedure.
2. Check the coil face for dirt and blocked fins.
3. Confirm the chilled-water valve opens as commanded.
4. Check the chilled-water supply temperature against design.
