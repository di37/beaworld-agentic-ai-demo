AIR FILTERS (FICTIONAL TRAINING MANUAL)

A dirty or clogged air filter restricts the air passing through the unit.
Typical symptoms: high filter differential pressure, low supply airflow, and
rooms that stay warmer than the setpoint even though the cooling coil is working.

Replacement interval: every 3 months, or sooner if the filter differential
pressure is HIGH.

Inspection steps for an engineer:
1. Isolate the unit using the site lockout procedure before opening any panel.
2. Check the filter bank for heavy dust loading, damage, or gaps where air bypasses the filter.
3. Compare the filter differential pressure with the clean-filter value on the unit label.
4. Replace the filter only with the grade specified for this unit.
